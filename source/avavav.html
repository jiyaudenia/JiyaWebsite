Filthy desires

The global sexual wellness market is booming, yet most brands focus on tech or function, leaving a gap for products that feel design-forward, inclusive, and unapologetically stylish. With its satire-driven, eco-conscious DNA, AVAVAV is perfectly primed to disrupt this space, bringing the same bold provocation it applies to fashion into one of the most taboo-breaking categories.


We propose Filthy Desires as AVAVAV’s extension into luxury sex toys: a line that merges playfulness, sustainability, and inclusivity with high-fashion aesthetics. From the Filthy Buzz, a vibrator inspired by the brand’s favicon, to the Filthy Drip, a wearable designed for LGBTQ+ audiences, the collection champions body autonomy and cheeky self-expression, delivered with AVAVAV’s signature irreverence.

takeaways

This project taught me how to translate brand DNA into a new product category.
