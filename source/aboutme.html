I’m a creative strategist pursuing my MA in Luxury and Brand Management at SCAD. With a focus on merchandising, buying, and cultural storytelling, I bring together academic insight, hands-on internships, and museum work to shape how luxury brands connect with people.

Originally from Rajasthan, born in Gujarat and raised in New Delhi, I’ve also lived in Sri Lanka, Qatar, Israel, and now the United States. Moving through these cultures has given me a perspective rooted in empathy, adaptability, and global curiosity.

Outside of work and school, I’m a cat mom to Ginger (rescued in Israel), a marathon finisher in Tel Aviv, and a big fan of live music, from Martin Garrix to The Weeknd. I also love cooking, reading, and staying active. And at my core, I’m what I like to call a “Barbie in the boardroom.”
