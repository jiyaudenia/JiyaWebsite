Crafted by nature

Tasked with re-envisioning Loewe's legacy, "Crafted by Nature" tells a story of purpose and transparency into Loewe's sustainability practices. Infusing the brand's iconic craftsmanship with a commitment to regenerative practices, this campaign aims to engage eco-conscious Millennials and Gen Z through immersive, multi-sensory experiences.
