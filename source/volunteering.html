CRY

With Child Rights and You (CRY), in March' 21 - 22, I had the opportunity to educate underprivileged children in India, creating interactive lessons that went beyond traditional classrooms and encouraged curiosity, confidence, and self-expression. This work was recognized by the national magazines of India, affirming the value of education as a tool for empowerment and change.
